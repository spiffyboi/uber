# Who's who (chapters 1 to 8)

The player is an unnamed night-shift cab driver in Marrow Heights.

The city has two crews, both made up for this game:
- **Kesting crew:** green. Their streets run from Harrow St down to Linden Ave.
- **Quarry crew:** maroon. Everything south of Linden.

TOLD / KEPT QUIET is the chapter 3 choice: whether you told Vance which house Dez went into.

## Dead

| Who | Who they were | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| **Tommy** | A kid who called from the payphone at Marrow Park (ch 1). Ran small jobs for the Quarry crew. | Robbed you at knifepoint at the wharf and took $40. Dez's people put him in hospital (ch 3, Vance: "eating through a straw"). Found dead behind the laundromat on Vail St (ch 4). Nobody says who did it. |
| **Curtis** | A barber on Quarry St, never seen up close. Gil's cousin. | Dez blamed him for Tommy's death ("made one phone call and went back to his dinner"). In ch 4 you waited outside while Dez went up to his lit window. Gunshots. Killed on his own stairs. |
| **Gil** | Fare from The Pit to Pier Park (ch 5). Delivery driver, owed Vance money and paid it off in favours. Father of Nina. | Vance sent him to kill you: as a loose end if you told, or for covering for Dez if you didn't. He held a gun to your head at the pier. You shot him with the pistol from the door panel. **Your first killing.** |
| **Dez** | Kesting crew, and your way in (ch 2 to 6). | Paid back the $40 Tommy took, and more. Put his number in your phone. Sent you on the house errand, the bag delivery, and the Quarry St night (he came out bleeding and you got him to Halo). In ch 6, during Lou's fare, you saw him shot in a side street. If you told Vance, it's his own crew, and he shouts your name before they fire. |

## Alive

| Who | Who they are | Where things stand |
|---|---|---|
| **Ray** | **Your very first fare** (ch 1), from Halcyon House to the bottom of Greaves Ave. | His daughter's school play is on Friday (she's a tree). His 19-year-old son was killed in crossfire between the crews last spring, walking home from the bodega. The family is moving upstate after Friday. He texts after Quarry St (ch 4: "Tell me that wasn't you") and after Gil (ch 5: "We're leaving Friday. There's room in the car."). **He's the way out.** |
| **Vance** | The Quarry crew's talker (ch 3). | Rode in your cab and asked which house Dez went into. Sent Gil after you. Keeps his mother on the 12th floor of Quarry Houses and visits Sundays (Teddy, ch 7). |
| **Teddy** | Gil's oldest friend. They drove bread trucks together for twenty years. | Helped you up when you collapsed at the funeral (ch 6). In ch 7 he made it clear he knows it was you, but he isn't going to the police. Wants a ride to Vance on **Friday, from the Lyric, at nine**: "Same thing you did. Except I'll mean it." |
| **Det. Calder** | Police detective (ch 7). Her card is in your phone. | Knows a yellow cab was seen at both killings. Asked where you were on Tuesday and whether you knew Dez (both answers are saved). Thinks someone's going to kill you. Texted: "Friday... He's called Teddy. Don't go anywhere with him." |
| **Nina** | Gil's daughter, about ten. | Played piano at his funeral (ch 6). Asked Teddy who the man was who fell down. |
| **Abe** | Owner of Ironside Arms on Ashby Ave (ch 5). | Sold you the pistol for $200, no paperwork. |
| **Lou** | A night-shift baker (ch 6). | Talked about rye bread the whole ride and saw nothing. |
| **June** | A nurse coming off a 16-hour shift (ch 8). | Fell asleep in the back. In the mirror, for a moment, she was Gil. Told you to call whoever you'd ask for at the end. |
| **Mr. Okafor** | An old man looking for a house with a blue door (ch 8). | Took you back to Pier Park. In the mirror, for a moment, he was Dez. Walked off along the railings and vanished. Maybe never there. |
| **The runner** | Kesting crew (ch 3). | Took Dez's bag off you at Rowan Houses. |
| **The minister** | Larkin & Sons (ch 6). | Led Gil's service. |

## Never seen

- **Mom:** texts in nearly every chapter, asking you to call. You never do.
- **Dispatch:** Marrow Cab Co. Sends the fares, and covered for you with the cops after Quarry St.
- **Unknown number:** Quarry's watchers, texting while you carried the bag (ch 3). If you told Vance, they text again after Calder gets out (ch 7).
- **The two men in the alley:** Dez's killers, never named.
