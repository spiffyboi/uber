## 2. Suggestions (not agreed, kept for when we build)

### Theme
- Teddy said "Same thing you did. Except I'll mean it." Instead the player kills him while trying to save him, the second man to die by the player's hand in the cab. It's Gil's scene again, inverted. Don't explain it; just let it land.

### Where it happens
- **Option A (recommended): Quarry Houses.** In chapter 7 Teddy says Vance keeps his mother on the 12th floor and visits Sundays.
  - "The house" becomes Vance's mother's flat, with his family photo on the wall.
  - "Footsteps from above" become men coming down the stairwell.
  - The escape is 12 floors of stairs: a chase.
- **Option B:** Vance's own house somewhere else. Teddy's chapter 7 line would need changing.
- Either way, Teddy needs one line on why it's Friday and not Sunday. For example: "He's moved it this week. His mother's birthday."

### Loose threads to pick up
- **Calder said "Don't go anywhere with him."** While you wait outside, her name buzzes on the dash (a missed call) and you let it ring. Later, with Teddy dying, you have her number: a ready-made choice for the next chapter.
- **Ray's family leaves on Friday too.** His text arrives during the monologue: "We're going tonight. There's still room in the car." It's the way out the player doesn't take.
- **The footsteps from above.** If one of the voices is Vance's, Part Two has its final confrontation.

### Making the player part of it
- **The shooting:** the aim shakes and the player presses to fire each shot. Whatever they do, 3 hit the man and the 4th hits Teddy. The player pulled the trigger.
- **The man walking in:** happens in normal play, not a cutscene, so the player spots him themselves. Then a prompt: "Go in."
- **The getaway:** a timed drive, like the Halo dash in chapter 4, then "pull over" once you're clear.

### The item: options
1. **Gil's cassette tape (recommended).** Gil recorded Vance ordering the hit on Curtis while he was paying off his debt. Calder wants it, Vance wants it, and you have it. It gives the rest of Part Two its spine.
2. **A key** to a locker at the bus depot, no explanation: a mystery object.
3. **An envelope for Nina:** "Make sure she gets this." An atonement mission: you'd have to face Gil's daughter.
4. **Vance's family photo**, with an address on the back. Leverage, and dangerous to hold.
- Options 1 and 3 work together: Teddy gives you both, and you decide what to do with each.

### Blood and Roblox's content rating
- Roblox rates games partly on how much realistic blood they show. "Covered in blood" could push the game to 17+.
- Keep it restrained: dark staining on his coat, red on the player's hands and the back seat. The dialogue does the rest. That should keep it at the Moderate rating.

### New things this needs built
- The cab driving itself through traffic in a cutscene, with the driver's hands turning the wheel (the hold-up scene's wheel-hand pose can be reused).
- A wing-mirror camera shot.
- Flashback flash-cuts:
  - The chapel set already exists (Nina, Teddy).
  - Gil in the back seat and the alley would be rebuilt as quick stand-ins.
- The inside of Vance's mother's flat, plus a stairwell. It would be a set under the street, like the chapel and the gun store.
- The shooting, with a shaking aim and four shots.
- Blood on Teddy and the seat.
- The final pull-out over the city. Distant buildings may not have loaded at that height, so it needs testing.
