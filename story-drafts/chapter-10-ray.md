## 4. Built (2026-10-19)

Chapter 10 is in the game, titled "Christmas Tree". As built:
- **Names (invented, easy to change):** Ray's wife Marie, his daughter Rosie, his son Danny.
- **Teddy's body:** at the start you say you left him on the steps of St. Brigid's and called it in from a payphone.
- **Ray's front step:** coffee, the play ("The wind is coming, Daddy"), the cloth for your hands, Danny. Then "I'll do the driving for once."
- **The drive:** Ray drives the cab to the new bus depot by the wharf, talking about Christmas and a tree farm.
- **The depot:** Rosie waves from the bus window. The gunman walks up the road in slow motion and fires four shots through the windscreen. Ray is killed.
- **The shooting:** you fall out of the passenger door and shoot back from the pavement, three shots with a shaking aim.
- **Who sent the gunman:** not revealed.
- **The end:** you walk while the edges of the screen close in, then look at your hand ("...That's not Teddy's.") and your shoulder. You collapse, it goes black, and a To Be Continued card says "There was room in the car."
