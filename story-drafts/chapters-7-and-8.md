# Uber — draft scripts: chapters 7 and 8

Working drafts. Edit anything: lines, names, places, order. Send it back and I'll build it.

**How to read this:**
- **[TEXT]**: a text arrives on the phone.
- **[PICKUP] / [RIDE] / [DROP-OFF]**: the usual fare steps.
- **[CHOICE]**: you pick what to say. The game remembers it.
- **[CUTSCENE]**: the camera takes over. Shot names in *italics*.
- **(beat)**: a pause with nothing said.
- **IF TOLD / IF QUIET**: only on that path (whether you told Vance about the house in chapter 3).
- Lines marked **(late)** are always said before the ride ends, even if you get there fast.

Places are real spots on the map. Swap them if you like.

## CHAPTER 8 — The Long Night

*You haven't slept in three days. A double shift, two fares, and the city starts to come apart at the edges. Some of it happens. Some of it doesn't. The game never says which.*

**Look and feel (things I'd build for this chapter):**
- **The mirror:** a new rear-view mirror shot, so we can cut to what's in the back seat.
- **Ghosts:** Gil and Dez can "appear" in the back seat for a shot or two and be gone in the next.
- **Weather:** heavy rain for the whole chapter, then it stops for the dawn at the end.
- **Dawn:** a short sunrise at the very end, the only daylight in the game so far.
- **No ghost speaks.** They just sit there. The fares talk.

### Opening

**[TEXT] Dispatch:** You've been on twenty hours. Go home.

**[TEXT] Dispatch:** ...Fine. Fare at Mercy General, the side entrance on Ashby. Nurse coming off nights.

|---|---|

| YOU | You don't get out. You just get further from where you went in. |

**[CUTSCENE] Dawn:**
- *outside*: the cab at the pier railings, rain easing.
- *driver*: on you. Eyes closing.
- **(black, a long beat)**
- *outside*: the same shot, but **daylight**: grey early sun on the water, the rain stopped, gulls. The first daylight in the game.
- *driver*: you wake up, stiff, in the driver's seat. Engine off. The meter's still running.
- *mirror*: the back seat. Empty. Just the empty seat. **(beat)**

**[TEXT] Mom:** Are you awake? I had a dream about you. Call me.

**[TEXT] Det. Calder:** Friday. The old man you drove. He's called Teddy. Don't go anywhere with him.
